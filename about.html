About Callsway Roof Co.

Built on Honesty, Not Sales Tactics

Callsway was started because too many homeowners were getting taken advantage of. Brent built the business he'd want to hire.

The Story

Hi, I'm Brent Call.

I've been working in roofing long enough to know how the industry can operate — big crews, subcontractors you've never met, salespeople who push you toward replacements you don't need. That's not how I want to run a business.

When I started Callsway, I made one rule: I'm on every job. Not a project manager, not a crew foreman you'll never see again. Me. I show up, I do the work, and I make sure it's done right before I leave.

We're based right here in Navasota. I live in the same community I serve, which means your neighbor might be my next customer. That keeps me honest.

If your roof doesn't need replacing, I'll tell you. If it does, I'll explain why in plain language, show you the damage, and give you a fair price. No pressure, no upselling, no nonsense.

When I'm not on a roof, you'll find me fishing, mountain biking, or spending time with my family. We're intentionally small — I'd rather do fewer jobs right than more jobs fast.
