About Callsway Roof Co.

Family-owned and based in Navasota. Brent Call built the roofing company he'd want to hire.

Hi, I'm Brent Call.

I've been in roofing long enough to know how the industry can work: big crews, subcontractors you've never met, and salespeople who push replacements you don't need. That's not how I want to run a business.

When I started Callsway, I made one rule: I'm on every job. You won't get a project manager or a foreman you never see again. I make sure the work is done right before I leave.

We're based here in Navasota. I live in the community I serve, so my next customer might be your neighbor. That keeps me honest.

If your roof doesn't need replacing, I'll tell you. If it does, I'll show you the damage, explain why in plain language, and give you a fair price.

When I'm not on a roof, I'm fishing, mountain biking, or spending time with my family. We're small on purpose. I'd rather do fewer jobs right than more jobs fast.
