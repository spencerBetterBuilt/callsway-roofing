Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 23, 2026

Callsway Roof Co. ("Callsway," "we," "us," or "our") operates callswayroofing.com (the "Site"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect through the Site, how we use it, and your options regarding that information.

Information We Collect

We collect information you voluntarily submit through the "Get a Free Estimate" forms on our Site, which may include:

  • Full name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • Details about your roofing project or service request

We do not use cookies, tracking pixels, or analytics tools on the Site at this time. If that changes in the future — for example, by adding Google Analytics to understand site traffic — we will update this policy to reflect it.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you submit to:

  • Respond to your estimate request and follow up about your project
  • Schedule inspections, estimates, or service calls
  • Communicate with you about work you've requested

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for their marketing purposes.

Third-Party Services

Our contact forms are processed by Web3Forms, a third-party form-delivery service that securely transmits your submission to our email inbox. Web3Forms processes this data solely to deliver it to us and does not use it for its own marketing. You can review Web3Forms' privacy policy for details on how they handle submitted data.

Data Retention

We retain the information you submit for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to your request and complete any related work, or until you ask us to delete it.

Your Rights

You may contact us at any time to ask what information we have about you, request a correction, or request that we delete it. To do so, email brent@callswayroofco.com or call (281) 221-5993.

Children's Privacy

The Site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, particularly as we add new tools or services to the Site. The "Last updated" date above reflects the most recent revision.
