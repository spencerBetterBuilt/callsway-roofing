Common Questions

Residential roofing FAQs

Answers to the questions homeowners ask us most before getting a new roof.

How long does a residential roof replacement take?

Most residential roof replacements are completed in a single day. Larger or more complex roofs may take two days. We'll give you a realistic timeline before we start so you can plan accordingly.

What type of shingles do you recommend?

We primarily install architectural (dimensional) shingles, which offer better durability and curb appeal than traditional 3-tab shingles. We carry multiple brands and styles and will help you choose the right product for your home and budget.

Do you handle permits for roof replacements?

Yes — permit requirements vary by city and county, and we handle all of that for you. You won't need to make a single call to the building department. We pull the permit, schedule any required inspections, and make sure everything is closed out properly.

What does the cleanup process look like after a roof replacement?

We take cleanup seriously. Our crew uses tarps to catch debris during tear-off, then we do a full sweep of the yard with a magnet to pick up any nails. We haul away all old shingles and materials the same day. You should barely know we were there — except for the new roof.
