Everything from tear-off to final walkthrough

A new roof is one of the biggest investments you'll make in your home. We use quality materials, tell you what we find, and respect your property while we work.

Residential roofing FAQs

Have a different question? Call Brent at (281) 221-5993.

How long does a residential roof replacement take?

Most residential roof replacements are completed in a single day. Larger or more complex roofs may take two days. We'll give you a realistic timeline before we start so you can plan around it.

What type of shingles do you recommend?

We primarily install architectural (dimensional) shingles, which offer better durability and curb appeal than traditional 3-tab shingles. We carry multiple brands and styles and will help you choose the right product for your home and budget.

Do you handle permits for roof replacements?

Yes. Permit requirements vary by city and county, and we handle all of that for you. We pull the permit, schedule any required inspections, and make sure everything is closed out properly.

What does the cleanup process look like after a roof replacement?

Our crew uses tarps to catch debris during tear-off, then sweeps the yard with a magnet to pick up nails. We haul away all old shingles and materials the same day.
