Storm damage and insurance FAQs

Have a different question? Call Brent at (281) 221-5993.

Will my homeowner's insurance cover storm damage to my roof?

Most homeowner's insurance policies cover storm damage, including hail, wind, and falling debris, as long as the damage isn't due to neglect or normal wear. What matters is documenting the damage properly and filing promptly. Call us for a free inspection and we'll tell you what we see before you open a claim.

How do I file a roof insurance claim after a storm?

Start by calling us for a free inspection. We document the damage with photos and a detailed report, then you file with your insurance carrier. Your adjuster will schedule a visit, and we can meet them on site to walk through our findings. Once the claim is approved, we handle the repair or replacement.

How quickly can you respond to storm damage in Navasota and surrounding areas?

We prioritize storm damage calls and typically respond within 24–48 hours of initial contact. If your roof is actively leaking, we can provide emergency tarping to protect your home while the inspection and claim are in progress.

What if my claim gets denied?

We can help you understand why the claim was denied and whether an appeal makes sense. We provide documentation (photos, measurements, and a written damage report) that can support a second review. We've helped homeowners successfully appeal denied claims.
