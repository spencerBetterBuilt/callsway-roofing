Common Questions

Gutter installation FAQs

Questions we hear from homeowners about gutters, sizing, and when to replace.

What size gutters do I need for my home?

Most residential homes use either 5-inch or 6-inch gutters. The right size depends on your roof pitch, the square footage draining to each section, and your local rainfall patterns. We assess your roof during a free estimate and recommend the correct size — oversized gutters aren't always better if they're not matched to proper downspout placement.

How often should gutters be replaced?

Seamless aluminum gutters typically last 20–30 years with proper maintenance. Signs it's time to replace include sagging sections, pulling away from the fascia, persistent leaks at seams, rust, or significant visible damage. If you're getting a new roof, it's often a good time to assess your gutters too.

Do gutters actually help protect my roof?

Yes — and your foundation, siding, and landscaping too. Without functioning gutters, water runs off the roof edge and can erode soil around your foundation, cause basement or crawlspace moisture issues, damage fascia and soffit, and accelerate wear on siding. Gutters are a small investment that protects a lot more than people realize.

Can you replace just a section of my gutters, or does it have to be the whole house?

We can repair or replace individual sections. We'll inspect what you have and give you an honest recommendation — sometimes a partial replacement makes sense, and sometimes replacing the full run is better value. We'll tell you which situation you're actually in.
