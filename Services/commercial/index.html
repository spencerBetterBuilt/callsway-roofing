Common Questions

Commercial roofing FAQs

Answers to questions we hear from commercial property owners and managers.

Do you handle commercial roofing projects of any size?

We take on select commercial projects — typically smaller to mid-size commercial buildings where we can give the project the same hands-on attention we give every residential job. We're not a volume shop. Call us and describe your project and we'll let you know if it's a good fit.

What types of commercial roofing do you install?

We specialize in flat roofing systems commonly used on commercial buildings, including TPO and modified bitumen. We'll assess your building, drainage needs, and budget to recommend the right system for your property.

How do you minimize disruption to my business during a roofing project?

We work with business owners to schedule the project in a way that minimizes impact on your operations. That may mean working in sections, scheduling certain phases during off-hours, or coordinating access points carefully. You'll always know the plan before we show up.

How long does a commercial roofing project take?

Timeline depends on the size and complexity of the project. Before we start, we provide a clear schedule with daily updates so you always know where we are in the process. Commercial projects typically range from one day to several days.
