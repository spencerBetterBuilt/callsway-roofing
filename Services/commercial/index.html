Commercial roofing FAQs

Have a different question? Call Brent at (281) 221-5993.

Do you handle commercial roofing projects of any size?

We take on select commercial projects, typically small to mid-size buildings where we can give the project the same hands-on attention we give every residential job. We're not a volume shop. Call us and describe your project, and we'll let you know if it's a good fit.

What types of commercial roofing do you install?

We specialize in flat roofing systems commonly used on commercial buildings, including TPO and modified bitumen. We'll assess your building, drainage needs, and budget to recommend the right system for your property.

How do you minimize disruption to my business during a roofing project?

We work with business owners to schedule the project around their operations. That may mean working in sections, scheduling certain phases during off-hours, or coordinating access points carefully. You'll know the plan before we show up.

How long does a commercial roofing project take?

It depends on the size and complexity of the project. Commercial projects typically range from one day to several days. Before we start, we give you a schedule, and we update you daily.
